Instagram Story Examples

Five sample stories built with the Pines Party Story Generator, using the weekend's colors, artwork and photos.

These are examples only. The brands below are made up to show what the tool can do. Some lean on photos, others on text slides. Every story opens with a co-branded card (your logo × Pines Party), ends on your logo, and each download comes with a short brief for our team.

About the link and poll boxes: the dashed box on each last slide shows where the Instagram sticker will sit. It's for display only. Instagram adds the real sticker when we post, so please send us your click-through URL (or poll question) and we'll attach it to your story.
